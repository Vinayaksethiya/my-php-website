Seven lead-generation landing pages (Auto Insurance, Health Insurance,
Medicare, Home Services, Pest Control, Final Expense, Water Services) in a
dark + cream + orange design. Each page has its own hero enquiry form plus a
popup quote form, wired to Contact Form 7 via Settings > Habitat LeadGen.

== Installation ==
1. Plugins > Add New: install and activate Contact Form 7.
2. Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme: upload habitat-leadgen.zip,
   then Activate.
3. Create the 7 pages (Pages > Add New) and assign each its
   "Habitat LeadGen" template.
4. Contact > Contact Forms: create 14 forms (hero + popup per page) and set
   each form's Mail tab To: address to your enquiry inbox.
5. Settings > Habitat LeadGen: paste the 14 shortcodes, plus your business
   name and phone number. Save.
6. Submit a test enquiry on each page and confirm the email arrives.
